About CampusBite

CampusBite is a fictional college food-ordering platform built as a technical club project. It's designed to show what a fast, friendly campus food-ordering experience could look like — from browsing the menu to picking up a hot order between classes.

This site is a demo: all stalls, items, ratings, reviews and orders are sample data created for demonstration purposes, not a real business.
